## LinkedIn announcement (draft)
I have finished my Data Analytics internship at ApexPlanet Software Pvt. Ltd. and published my portfolio.

Over four tasks I worked with one retail sales dataset of 1,000 transactions:
1. Cleaned and audited the raw data in Python.
2. Explored it and answered seven business questions with SQL.
3. Defined KPIs, segmented orders and prepared a Power BI dashboard design with DAX.
4. Tested six business hypotheses statistically.

My biggest lesson: five of my six hypotheses were not statistically significant, including city and category differences that looked meaningful on charts. Testing before recommending changed how I present results.
