About me

I am Mohammed Farshan, a data analyst who completed the ApexPlanet Software Pvt. Ltd. Data Analytics Internship.

Over four connected tasks I worked on one retail sales dataset of 1,000 transactions: I cleaned it, explored it with Python and SQL, designed a KPI dashboard and then checked my own findings with statistical tests. That last step taught me the most. Several differences that looked meaningful in charts, such as order value by city, turned out to be within normal variation.

I like analysis that ends in a clear recommendation, and I try to say plainly what the data can and cannot support.
